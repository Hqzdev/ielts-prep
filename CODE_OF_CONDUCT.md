# Veylo Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Veylo welcomes participants of every background and experience level. Treat people with dignity across issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces where you represent the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect differences, accessibility needs and personal boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, targeted insults, doxxing and retaliation against reporters are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the repository maintainer privately through [Telegram](https://t.me/fucktheperson), as listed on [Hqzdev's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Hqzdev). Include relevant links, dates and a brief account of the incident.

Do not post private reports, learner answers, recordings, credentials or personal information in public issues.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports fairly and limits disclosure to what is necessary to address the concern. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include removing content, a warning, participation restrictions or a temporary or permanent ban. Retaliation can result in further restrictions.

## Reference

These standards draw on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
